Visualizer Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026

Visualizer (“the App”) helps you create motivational lock-screen wallpapers of your future self. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have.

Who we are

Visualizer is made by Redastic Labs, the trading name of an independent developer based in New Delhi, India. Redastic Labs is not yet a registered company. “We”, “us” and “our” in this policy mean Redastic Labs. Contact: redastics@gmail.com. The App’s servers are at api.redastic.com.

Short version: Your account and dream (goal) details are stored on our servers. Your photos stay on your phone and are not stored on our servers, but when you make a wallpaper they are sent from your phone to Google Gemini to create the image. The face check that makes sure your photos show one person, and the same person, runs only on your phone and saves nothing. We do not sell your personal information. You can delete your account at any time in the App.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account identifier, email address, display name, and profile photo URL (if Google provides one). We store these to create and authenticate your account, along with a record of your signed-in devices (sessions).
  • Profile and dream details. Optional profile fields (such as name, age and gender) and your dream details (title, why it matters, desired results, habits, start date) are stored on our servers so we can personalize wallpaper prompts. If you describe a health, weight or fitness goal, that text is treated as health or fitness information for that purpose.
  • Your photos. Photos you add are stored only on your phone. Use photos of yourself only: not other people, and not anyone under 18. We do not upload or store your photo files on our servers. When you make a wallpaper, your selected photos are sent from your phone to Google’s Gemini API solely to create it.
  • Face data. The App checks your photos on your phone to make sure each one shows one face and that they all show the same person. See “Face data” below.
  • Face data consent. When you agree on the App’s face data screen, we store which version of that notice you agreed to and when. If you withdraw, we erase that record.
  • Generated wallpapers. Images created by Gemini are AI-generated visualizations. They are saved on your phone, labeled as AI-generated in the App, and not uploaded to our servers.
  • Wallpaper requests. When you make a wallpaper, the App asks our servers for a prompt and tells us whether the wallpaper was created. We keep a record of each request (time, whether it was manual or automatic, and whether it worked) to apply generation limits. To size and style wallpapers, the App also sends device and display details (for example manufacturer, model, screen size and theme colors). Photos are never part of these requests.
  • Automatic wallpaper status. While the App is open it sends our servers whether automatic wallpapers are on, how often they run, and whether your phone keeps stopping them in the background (a missed-run count and a paused flag). Together with the times of automatic wallpaper requests, we use this to email you if automatic wallpapers stop. It contains no photos or dream text.
  • Subscription and trial records. If you subscribe to Visualizer Plus, Google Play takes the payment; we never receive your card or other payment details. We store your Google Play purchase token, plan (monthly or yearly), subscription status, renewal or end date, and Google’s order number with your account. We also store when your free trial started and ends.
  • Usage analytics and crash diagnostics. The App uses Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring to understand which features are used and to fix crashes and slow screens. These signals include your internal user ID on crash reports and a Firebase app-instance ID, and never include your photos. Advertising ID collection is turned off.
  • Feedback and content reports. Messages you send through Feedback, and reports you submit when you flag an AI-generated visualization, are stored and/or emailed to us so we can support you and moderate generated content. A successful report also removes that visualization from your gallery on your phone.
  • Website visits. The website redastic.com is static and needs no account. It has no analytics; our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log basic technical data such as IP address and browser type.

2. Face data

  • What the face check does. When you add photos, the App uses on-device face detection (Google ML Kit) and an on-device face-matching model to check that each photo shows one face and that all your photos show the same person. This creates a temporary face measurement in your phone’s memory.
  • Never saved, never sent. That face measurement is used only for the check, is discarded right after, is never written to storage, and never leaves your phone. We do not receive it, store it, sell it, or share it with anyone.
  • Photos and Google Gemini. To create each wallpaper, your photos are sent from your phone to Google’s Gemini API, which we use under a paid account. Under Google’s current terms for paid Gemini API use, Google does not use your photos or prompts to improve its products. Google may keep them for a limited time to detect abuse, as described in Google’s Gemini API terms.
  • Your consent. Before you add photos, the App asks you to confirm that you are 18 or older, that the photos are of you, and that you agree to this use of your face data. You can’t add photos without agreeing.
  • Withdrawing. You can withdraw at any time in Profile → Your photos → Remove all photos. This deletes your photos from the App and erases your consent record. Deleting your account does the same.

3. How we use information

  • Sign you in and maintain your account
  • Build prompts and create personalized wallpapers
  • Apply generation limits and prevent abuse
  • Confirm Visualizer Plus purchases with Google Play and decide whether you can create new wallpapers
  • Email you once if your phone keeps stopping automatic wallpapers, and not again until they run again or you open the App. Every such email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Improve the App using analytics, crash reports, feedback and content reports
  • Answer your support and privacy requests

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We share data only with these providers, to run the App:

  • Google Sign-In signs you in, under Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini. When you make a wallpaper, your photos and the prompt (including your dream text, which may include health or fitness details) are sent from your phone to Google’s Gemini API. See “Face data” above.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring) processes usage, crash and performance data.
  • Google Play handles purchases and billing. To confirm a purchase and keep your subscription status current, our servers send its purchase token to Google Play’s developer API.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) hosts our servers and database in Mumbai, India. If you live outside India, your account data is stored and processed in India.
  • Resend delivers our email: Feedback, content reports and alerts to our team, and the automatic-wallpaper email to you, using the email address from your Google sign-in.
  • Legal authorities, when the law requires it or to protect rights, safety and security.

5. Data retention

  • Your photos and generated wallpapers stay on your phone until you remove them or delete your account in the App (which also clears them from the App). If Android Backup or device transfer is on, they may also be included so a reinstall can restore your gallery (sign-in tokens are not backed up).
  • The text of each wallpaper prompt is erased from our servers after a short period (currently 24 hours). The record that a request happened, without the prompt, stays with your account to apply generation limits.
  • Account, profile, dream, face consent, wallpaper request, subscription, feedback and content-report records are kept while your account exists and are deleted from our active database when you delete your account. Email deletion requests are handled by hand after we confirm the request came from the Google email address you sign in with. Deleted records may remain in protected backups for up to 30 days before they are overwritten.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; Google keeps its own billing records. If you started a free trial, we keep a one-way (SHA-256) hash of your Google account ID with your trial start and end dates, not linked to your account, so a new account on the same Google account does not get a second free trial.
  • If you choose to share a reason when deleting your account, that reason may be kept after deletion, not linked to your account, so we can improve the App. When you delete your account in the App, we may also email ourselves a summary of the wallpaper activity on your phone (including prompts and errors) to understand why people leave.
  • Emails already delivered to our mailbox (account-deletion correspondence, Feedback, content reports and the deletion summary above) are not deleted with your account and may be kept indefinitely.

6. Your choices

  • Add, replace or remove your photos at any time in Profile → Your photos
  • Update your profile and dream details in the App
  • Withdraw face data consent with Profile → Your photos → Remove all photos
  • Permanently delete your account in Profile → Delete account, or request deletion by email
  • Flag an offensive AI-generated visualization from its preview without leaving the App (this also removes it from your gallery)
  • Turn off the automatic-wallpaper email with the unsubscribe link in it
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play (Profile → Manage Plus in the App)

7. Your US privacy rights

Wherever you live in the United States, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy of it
  • correct personal information that is wrong
  • delete your personal information
  • stop selling or sharing your personal information for targeted advertising. We don’t do either, so there is nothing to opt out of.

To make a request, email redastics@gmail.com from the Google email address you sign in with, or delete your account in the App. We will answer within 45 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. You can use an authorized agent if you give them written permission. We will not treat you differently, charge you more or give you a worse service for using these rights.

8. Your rights in India

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to give you a summary of your personal data and how we use it, correct or complete it, erase it, and withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn’t affect what we did before you withdrew. You can also nominate another person to use these rights for you if you die or can no longer act yourself.

Grievance contact: Redastic Labs, New Delhi, India, redastics@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Children

The App is only for people aged 18 or older (or the minimum age in your country, if higher). You must confirm you are 18 or older before signing in. Do not upload photos of anyone under 18 or ask the App to create images of children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

10. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers uses HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Keep your Google account protected.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The dates at the top show when this version took effect and when it was last changed. If we make a material change, we will tell you in the App before it takes effect. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: email redastics@gmail.com, use Send feedback in the App’s Profile tab, or contact us through the Visualizer listing on Google Play (com.visualizer.app).
