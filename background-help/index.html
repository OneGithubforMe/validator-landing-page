Keep automatic wallpapers running

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Visualizer makes new wallpapers on a schedule, even when the app is closed. Many phones stop apps in the background to save battery. When that keeps happening, Visualizer pauses automatic wallpapers and lets you know. Allow background activity once and they resume on their own.

Quickest fix: open Visualizer and tap Fix and resume (or Fix it) on the popup. It opens the right settings screen for your phone.

Any Android phone

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Visualizer.
  2. Tap Battery (or App battery usage).
  3. Turn on Allow background usage, then choose Unrestricted.
  4. If Data Saver is on, also allow Unrestricted data usage for Visualizer.

Some phones also stop apps when you swipe them away from the recent-apps screen. If updates stop after that, lock Visualizer in Recents.

Samsung

  1. Open Visualizer's app info, then Battery.
  2. Choose Unrestricted.
  3. In Settings → Battery → Background usage limits, add Visualizer to Never sleeping apps.

Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO

  1. Open Visualizer's app info, then Battery saver.
  2. Choose No restrictions.
  3. Turn on Autostart for Visualizer.

OPPO, Realme, OnePlus

  1. Open Visualizer's app info, then Battery (or Power consumption).
  2. Choose Allow background activity, not Smart mode.
  3. Turn on Autostart (or Allow auto launch) if you see it.

vivo

  1. Open i Manager or Battery.
  2. Allow high background power consumption for Visualizer.
  3. Turn on Autostart if you see it.

Huawei, Honor

  1. Open Settings → Battery → App launch.
  2. Find Visualizer, turn off Manage automatically, and allow it to run in the background.
  3. On older versions, add Visualizer to Protected apps.

About the email

If your phone keeps Visualizer closed for several of your scheduled updates in a row, we send one email to your Google sign-in address so you know. We don't send another until you open the app again. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. See the Privacy Policy for what the app shares to make this work.
