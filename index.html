FAQ

What is Visualizer?

An Android app from Redastic that creates motivational lock-screen wallpapers of your future self from your photos and your goal.

Where are my photos stored?

On your phone. We do not upload or permanently store those files on our servers. When you generate a wallpaper, selected photos are sent from your device to Google Gemini. Android Backup or a device transfer may restore local media.

Will it replace my current wallpaper?

Yes, on the screens you allow — lock, home, or both. You set that during setup and can change it later in Settings.

Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in with Google so we can save your goal and account. You must be 13 or older.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. You can also request deletion by email from the data deletion page.

Is this a health or medical app?

No. If you type a health, weight, or fitness goal, that text is used only to personalize wallpaper prompts — not as medical advice or Health Connect data.
